# Unified Lead Forms & Social Accounts

- Home, Contact, Apply Now, College/Course, AI Chatbot, WhatsApp Chatbot, Facebook Messenger and Instagram Messenger use the same compact lead form fields.
- Every valid form requires consent and creates/updates a Lead CRM record using the mobile number for deduplication.
- Lead source is stored in CRM for reporting.
- Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn public buttons use Admissions Administration > Social links. Add the official account/channel URL there; clicks are tracked through SocialClick.
- Facebook Messenger / Instagram Messenger buttons first open the lead form so the enquiry is captured before counselling follow-up.
- YouTube button opens the configured channel so visitors can subscribe; Instagram/Facebook open the configured profile/page so visitors can follow.
