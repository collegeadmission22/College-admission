# College CRM / Search Update

- Removed Course Category from the Colleges search filter.
- Search placeholder is now: `Search Best Colleges and Courses here`.
- College filter now uses College Category + State.
- College categories: Medical, Management, Law, Engineering, Media, International.
- College Admin is simplified to match Course Admin: College Name, University Name, College Category, State, career content, uploaded image and SEO only.
- Apply Now / Get Free Counselling posts directly to the Lead CRM through `quick_apply`.
- The enquiry form now captures City, Course, Preferred College and consent; clicking a college/course Apply button preselects that item.
- Existing hidden database fields were not deleted, preventing unnecessary data loss; they are removed from the College Admin editing form.

After replacing the project, run:
`python manage.py migrate`
then:
`python manage.py runserver`
